Focus Guard

Privacy

Focus Guard is designed to make a decision about the page you are actively viewing, without building a browsing history.

What is sent

For an active focus session, the extension sends your focus statement and limited current-page metadata: URL, title, page type, and short public page context. This is needed for JEV to decide whether the page supports your focus.

Bring your own key

When you select Cloudflare, TypeSafe, or a compatible provider, that information goes directly from the extension to that provider. Focus Guard does not receive it.

Hosted access

Hosted mode relays the same request through the Focus Guard service. The service does not store page URLs, titles, page metadata, model responses, cookies, or browsing history. It retains only minimal pseudonymous device and quota data needed to prevent abuse and enforce the free allowance.

Local extension data

Your provider settings, focus session, and short-lived decision cache remain in your browser profile. They are not synced or sent to Focus Guard except as described above.

No sale or advertising

Focus Guard does not sell personal data, run advertising, or use your browsing data to train models.
